# Code of Conduct

Rasputin is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming
community.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good faith while remaining precise about evidence and risk.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Respect privacy, security embargoes, and confidential vulnerability reports.
- Credit upstream work and other contributors.
- Accept maintainers' responsibility to protect users and repository safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private data, or unpatched
  vulnerabilities.
- Plagiarism or removal of required attribution.
- Repeated disruption of technical discussion or review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, limit
participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.
Reports should use the project's private security-reporting channel when they
contain sensitive information.

Enforcement details and a dedicated community contact will be expanded before
the first public release.
